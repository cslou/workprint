Flow 2 · Create a scene

A new user, with nothing saved yet, asks for a short scene of three shots.

  1. 1Opening screenThe agent asks what you'd like to makeApproved
  2. 2Describe the sceneThe agent drafts the script with a shot breakdown, and asks questionsApproved
  3. 3Characters, places and propsFor each: upload photos, describe it, or have it designedApproved
  4. 4Choose the looksThree looks for each described item; pick oneApproved
  5. 5Approve the reference sheetsAll sheets togetherApproved
  6. 6Plan the storyboardPanels and camera per shot, in one approvalApproved
  7. 7Approve the storyboardCheck the panels; edit in the board and regenerateApproved
  8. 8Make the videosOne video per shot, in one approvalApproved
  9. 9Choose the takesPlay the scene in order; choose or redo a shotApproved
  10. 10Compare versionsA new version of shot 2; watch both in the scene and chooseApproved

After choosing versions, continue to the current edit in Videos and the Editor. The Editor flow and state reference marks unfinished editing/export behaviour explicitly.
