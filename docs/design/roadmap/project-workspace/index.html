Scene script

Vale and Mercer meet

What happens

Mercer and Vale sit side by side on the gallery bench. Vale realises who the young man is and quietly speaks; Mercer reacts.

Shots
  1. Vale and Mercer on the bench

    Both of them, seen together from the front: Mercer on the left, Vale on the right, facing the painting. Neither speaks.

  2. Vale recognises Mercer

    Close on Vale. He studies the young man beside him, realises who he is, and speaks quietly.

    Vale: “You must be Mercer.”

  3. Mercer reacts

    Close on Mercer as he takes in being recognised. He stays still, then gives a small nod.

About 13 seconds in all.
